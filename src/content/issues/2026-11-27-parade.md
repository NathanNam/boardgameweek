The Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade turns 100 this year. It was meant to march on
Thursday, but with heavy rain and strong winds forecast for New York on Thanksgiving
Day, the city, Macy's and NBC moved it to Saturday, Nov. 28, and NBC showed last
year's parade on Thursday instead. On Saturday it travels 2.5 miles from the Upper
West Side to Macy's Herald Square, with a record 100 balloons and other inflatables,
new floats and a visit from Santa. It's live on NBC and Peacock from 8:30 a.m. Eastern,
hosted by Savannah Guthrie, Hoda Kotb and Al Roker, with a Spanish-language broadcast
on Telemundo.

In *Parade*, the characters from *Alice's Adventures in Wonderland* are marching,
and nobody wants to be left holding them. On your turn you add a card to the end of
the line, and depending on its number and colour, some of the cards already in the
parade walk off into your pile, where they count against you. Lowest score wins. It
takes five minutes to learn, has no words on the cards and plays with up to six, so
it suits a long weekend with relatives of every age.
