Formula 1 comes to the Las Vegas Strip this weekend for round 21 of the season. It's
a night race: 50 laps of a 3.853-mile street circuit, with a run of nearly two
kilometres down the Strip past the big resorts. The race starts Saturday at 8 p.m.
Pacific, 11 p.m. Eastern, and it's on Apple TV in the US. The title is already
decided (Kimi Antonelli clinched it in Mexico and is the youngest champion in the
sport's history), but the fight for second place is tight, with Max Verstappen just
ahead of four other drivers.

In *Heat: Pedal to the Metal* each player drives an old-school race car with a hand
of cards. Play high cards to go fast, but take a corner too quickly and you pay with Heat
cards that clog your hand, or spin out. It's quick to teach, it
plays well with four, and the races are often close right to the finish. Play one at dinner,
then stay up for the real thing.
