Halloween falls on a Saturday this year, so trick-or-treating, costumes and parties
all land on the weekend. The National Retail Federation expects a record
$13.5 billion in Halloween spending, with more going on costumes than on candy.
And on Saturday night the clocks go back: daylight saving time ends at 2 a.m. on
Sunday, Nov. 1. An extra hour after a night out in costume is the perfect excuse
for one more game.

In *Mysterium* a ghost haunts an old manor and wants to tell you who did it, where
and with what. The catch: the ghost can't talk. One player is the ghost and
silently hands out dreamlike picture cards; everyone else is a psychic trying to
read them. It's cooperative, so you win or lose together, and it's spooky without
being scary. The best part is the table talk: "Why would the ghost send me a
lighthouse?"
