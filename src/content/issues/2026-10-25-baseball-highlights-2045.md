The World Series is on. The Milwaukee Brewers won the first two games at home,
3–2 and 5–4, and the series moves to Tampa Bay to face the Rays for Game 3 on Monday. Two teams,
two one-run games, and a whole country arguing about lineups and pitching
changes: the perfect week to let your kids be the manager.

In *Baseball Highlights: 2045* each player runs a team. You play short "mini-games"
of six cards each, then sign free agents to make your team better before the next
one. It's quick to learn, every game is close, and it rewards exactly the
decisions you'll hear argued about during the broadcast: who to put in, who to
save for later, and when to take a risk.
