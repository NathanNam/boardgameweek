The Leonid meteor shower is active through November, and it peaks on the night of
Tuesday, Nov. 17, into Wednesday morning. The meteors are dust from Comet
Tempel–Tuttle, hitting the air at about 70 kilometres a second. Expect 10 to 15
an hour under a dark sky, best after midnight once the Moon has set. This weekend
the Moon is a crescent that sets by mid-evening, so a clear night is already a good
excuse to go outside, look up and count.

In *SETI: Search for Extraterrestrial Intelligence* each player runs a space
program. You launch probes to the planets, point radio telescopes at the sky,
analyse the data and race to find the first signs of alien life, and the aliens
you discover change the game as it goes. It's a big, brainy game for a teen and
an adult (or a 12-year-old with some help), and the best part is the moment a
species is revealed and everyone has to work out what it means.
